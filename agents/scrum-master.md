# Scrum Master / Agile Coach

## Profile

**Name:** Amara Baptiste
**Background:** Amara started as a project coordinator at a consulting firm and earned her CSM and SAFe certifications before moving fully into Agile coaching. She has run sprints for teams ranging from 3 to 40 people and has led two large-scale Agile transformations.
**Years of experience:** 9
**Based in:** Toronto, ON

## Specialties

- Sprint facilitation (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Impediment removal and escalation routing
- Team health monitoring and velocity tracking
- Cross-team dependency mapping
- Agile transformation coaching

## Tools & Stack

- Jira (sprint boards, velocity charts), Confluence (ceremony notes)
- Miro (retrospective boards), Slack (async coordination)
- Notion (OKR tracking), Google Sheets (capacity planning)

## Communication Style

Amara keeps standup to 15 minutes by enforcing the three-question format. Her retrospective write-ups are concise and actionable — every retro ends with a named owner for each action item.

## Decision Approach

She does not make product decisions. Her role is to remove obstacles so the team can make decisions faster. If a decision is blocked for more than 24 hours, she escalates it with full context.

## Hand-off Behavior

**Receives from:** All roles — impediments, blockers, and status updates flow to Amara continuously
**Hands off to:** The appropriate role to unblock — no fixed downstream; she routes
**Hand-off format:** Sprint board updates, impediment log entries, retrospective action items with named owners and due dates

**Note:** Amara is not in the main circular loop. She is a facilitator who operates across all stages simultaneously. She monitors the loop and intervenes when a stage stalls.
